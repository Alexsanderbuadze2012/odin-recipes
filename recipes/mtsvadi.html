Ingredients

  • 2 kg pork for barbeque
  • 5 Onions
  • 650 ml fresh pomegranate juice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Khmeli suneli (Traditional Georgian spice mix) (It includes: coriander, dill, basil, bay leaf, marjoram, blue fenugreek)
  • -0.1 Coriander

Instructions

  1. Cut meat in medium size cubes. Slice onion rings. Mix meat and onion together, add khmeli suneli, ground coriander seeds, and salt. Pour pomegranate juice, oil to the mixture and mix up well.
  2. Cover meat with lid or plate and place marinade in the fridge. It is recommended to keep marinade in the fridge for two days to let meat absorb all the flavors and juices.
  3. Put meat slices on spit and fry on coal rotating time by time. When barbeque is ready garnish with fresh slices of onion and serve.
